1. Objective

Develop a comprehensive POS Dashboard in MAC One that provides users with a real-time overview of branch operations, sales performance, Work Orders, cash register status, and inventory.

The dashboard shall display information based on the logged-in user's permissions, assigned branch, terminal, and active working session.

As a POS user , I want to view a dashboard containing sales, invoices, Work Orders, inventory, cash register, and financial summaries, so that I can monitor branch performance and identify operational or financial issues without navigating through multiple screens.

3. Dashboard Navigation

The POS Dashboard shall contain the following main tabs:

  1. Overview

  2. Sales

  3. WO Info

  4. Cash

  5. Inventory

The system shall display the Overview tab by default when the user opens the dashboard.

Each tab shall display relevant KPIs, charts, lists, and summaries.

The user shall be able to navigate from a KPI or dashboard component to the corresponding detailed screen or filtered transaction list, subject to permissions.

5.4 Expected Cash Balance

The Expected Cash Balance shall represent the physical cash expected in the cash drawer.

Visa, Mada, and other electronic payment methods shall not be included in the Expected Cash Balance.

Note that: 

Expected cash = 

Opening Cash
&+ Cash Sales
&+ Cash In (Not Supported)
&+ Cash Transfers In (Not Supported)
&- Cash Refunds
&- Cash Expenses (Not Supported)
&- Cash Deposits
&- Cash Transfers Out (Not Supported)
&+ Cash Adjustments Increase (Not Supported)
&- Cash Adjustments Decrease (Not Supported)

Important Rules

  1. Cash sales increase the expected cash balance.

  2. Visa and Mada sales do not affect the expected cash balance.

  3. Cash refunds decrease the expected cash balance, regardless of the original invoice payment method.

  4. Visa and Mada refunds do not affect the physical cash balance.

  5. Cash expenses, deposits, and transfers out decrease the expected cash balance. [Cash expenses/transfers out not supported]

  6. Cash in and transfers in increase the expected cash balance. [not supported]

  7. Only confirmed cash movements associated with the selected session shall be included.

8.4 Gross Profit


Gross Profit=Net Sales - COGS

Where:


Net Sales=Gross Sales-Discounts-Sales Returns

COGS =sum(Net Quantity Sold X Applicable Unit Cost

Example

DescriptionAmount
Gross Sales 10,000 JOD
Discounts 500 JOD
Sales Returns 300 JOD
Cost of Goods Sold 5,000 JOD

Net Sales=10,000-500-300=9,200 JOD

Gross Profit=9,200-5,000=4,200 JOD

8.5 Gross Margin (Excluding Tax)
Gross Margin =Gross Profit ÷ Net Sales × 100

Example:

Gross Margin={4,200}÷{9,200} X100=45.65%

If Net Sales = 0, display N/A or 0% 

8.6 Output VAT

Output VAT represents the VAT charged on eligible sales transactions.

Output VAT =Sales Output VAT - Sales Return VAT

Example:

Output VAT=1,500-100=1,400 JOD

8.7 Input VAT

Input VAT represents recoverable VAT from eligible purchase transactions.

Input VAT = Recoverable Purchase VAT - Purchase Return VAT

Example:

Input VAT=700-50=650 JOD

8.8 Net VAT Payable

Net VAT Payable=Output VAT- Input VAT

Period Selection and Date Filters

Predefined Periods

The system shall provide the following predefined period options:

OptionDescription
Daily Display transactions for the current business day.
MTD Month to Date – from the first day of the current month until the current business date.
QTD Quarter to Date – from the first day of the current quarter until the current business date.
YTD Year to Date – from the first day of the current financial year until the current business date.

Default Selection: Daily.

Custom Date Range

The system shall provide:

  • From Date.

  • To Date.

  • Apply button.

Business Rules

  1. Daily, MTD, QTD, and YTD shall automatically populate the From and To dates.

  2. The user can select a custom date range using the date pickers.

  3. Clicking Apply shall refresh all KPIs, transaction counts, and transaction records based on the selected date range.

  4. The From Date must be less than or equal to the To Date.

  5. Changing the predefined period shall update the date range and refresh the displayed data.

  6. If the user manually changes either date, the system shall treat the selection as a custom date range.

  7. The selected date range shall remain unchanged when switching between All, Refunds, and Invoices.

  1. Total Sales shall include all confirmed sales invoices regardless of payment method.

  7. Each invoice shall be counted only once, regardless of the number of payment records associated with it.

Average Invoice

Description:

Display the average net sales value per confirmed sales invoice during the selected period.

Average Invoice = {Sales Amount} ÷ {Number of Confirmed Invoices}

  • Confirmed Invoices = 50.

Average Invoice = 4,500 ÷ 50 = 90 JOD

Business Rules:

  3. If the invoice count is zero, display 0.00.

Refunds and Return Rate

Description:

Display the total value of confirmed sales refunds/returns and the return rate for the selected period.

The KPI card shall display:

  • Refunds Amount.

  • Return Rate Percentage.

Refunds Amount:

Refunds Amount =sum Confirmed Sales Return Net Amount

Return Rate:

Return Rate% ={Refunds Amount} ÷ {Gross Sales} X 100 %

Example:

  • Gross Sales = 5,000 JOD.

  • Confirmed Refunds/Returns = 200 JOD.


Return Rate = {200}÷{5,000}X100% = 4%

The system shall display:

  • Refunds: 200 JOD.

  • Return Rate: 4.0%.

Business Rules:

  1. Only confirmed sales returns/refunds shall be included.

  2. Draft, cancelled, and rejected return documents shall be excluded.

  3. If Gross Sales is zero, display 0.0% and avoid division by zero.

  4. The KPI shall be based on the sales return document date, not the original invoice date.

  5. The KPI shall be based on the sales return document date, not the original invoice date.
